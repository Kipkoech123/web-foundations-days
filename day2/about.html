About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for capturing your thoughts the moment you have them. It helps students and busy people keep ideas, reminders, and to-do items in one easy place.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note into the "New note" box.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button.
  3. Find your note in the "Your notes" list.
  4. Come back any time to read your notes again.

Features

  • Add a note in seconds
  • See all your notes in one list
  • Keep a count of how many notes you have
  • Works on phones and computers

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Tab Move to the next field or button
Enter Add the note you typed
Shift + Tab Move back to the previous field
